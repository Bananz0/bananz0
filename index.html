  5. IC designHardware / Verilog2023–2025

    TSMC 65nm Real-Time Clock

    Led the team taking a ring-oscillator real-time clock from RTL to tested silicon in TSMC 65nm. Schematics in S-Edit, SPICE in T-Spice, layout in L-Edit, and DRC/LVS-clean in Calibre after cutting the layout area by 15%. For test we extracted a Verilog golden model from the schematics, wrote functional and timing vectors, and ran them on the fabricated chip on an IC tester: every vector passed.

    65nm process 0 DRC errors −15% area 100% test pass

    S-Edit / T-Spice / L-Edit / Calibre DRC/LVS / Verilog / IC Testing

    Group project, team lead

Interrupt-driven, with sub-millisecond response. Demonstrated as part of the IC testing coursework, covering chip validation and system integration.

  7. Computer architectureC++2025

    MIPSquare++: 5-Stage Pipelined MIPS Simulator

    A 5-stage pipelined MIPS simulator (IF/ID/EX/MEM/WB) in C++23. It has its own assembler with label resolution for R-, I- and J-type instructions, EX/MEM and MEM/WB forwarding, load-use stalls and branch flushing. The demo program computes the squares of 0–200 by summing odd numbers and runs at close to 1 CPI, with tests covering RAW and control hazards.

    5 pipeline stages ~1.0 CPI 17 instructions

    C++23 / CMake / Data Forwarding / Hazard Detection / MIPS ISA
