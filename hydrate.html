HYDRATE

STAY HYDRATED, PRIVATELY

{% include legal-nav.html %}

Hydrate is a hydration tracker for Android. Log water in a single tap, follow a goal tailored to you, and synchronise automatically with your smart bottle, Health Connect and Samsung Health. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Personal daily goal

A recommended intake based on your profile and activity, which you can adjust at any time.

Clear statistics

Progress, pace, streaks, weekly averages and a 30-day overview at a glance.

Smart-bottle sync

Connects to compatible smart bottles over Bluetooth to record every drink automatically.

Health Connect and Samsung Health

Keeps your hydration records consistent across the health apps you already use.

Reminders

Optional reminders on your phone or bottle, limited to the days and hours you choose.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. The developer never receives your log or profile.

Privacy and legal

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use, or learn how to access and delete your data. Questions about privacy: privacy@glenmuthoka.com.

Hydrate is a general wellness app and not a medical device. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with any bottle manufacturer.
